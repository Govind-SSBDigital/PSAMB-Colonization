Introduction / ਭੂਮਿਕਾ

Punjab State Land & Property Colonization Board (Colonization)

Establishing, colonizing, and transforming rural & semi-urban commercial markets into advanced trade hubs.

The Colonization Department functions as the core property management and development organ of the Punjab State Land & Property Colonization Board (PSLPCB). Initiated under the Colonization of Government Lands Act, the department is responsible for the systematic acquisition, planning, allotment, and administration of commercial mandi booths, residential plots, and shop sites.

Through transparency and digital-first services like online property ledger cards, digital mutation tracking, and e-Auctions, we are enabling direct, hassle-free interfaces for all citizens, traders, and commission agents in the state of Punjab.
